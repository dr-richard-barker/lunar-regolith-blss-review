\section{Conclusions}
\label{sec:conclusions}

Direct edaphic cultivation in raw lunar regolith presents fundamental biological, toxicological, and fluid-mechanical barriers that cannot be resolved through chemical fertilization alone\cite{paul2022plants,ming1989lunar,lytvynenko2021lunar}. Molecular, transcriptomic, and toxicological investigations confirm that pristine lunar dust contains reactive nanophase metallic iron ($\mathrm{npFe}^0$), unpassivated silicate dangling bonds, and razor-sharp mineral shards that trigger chronic oxidative stress cascades, inflict 8-oxoguanine genomic lesions, accelerate telomere shortening, and arrest plant root development\cite{paul2022plants,ming1989lunar,hendrix2024reactivity,barbero2024telomere}. Simultaneously, the reduction of gravitational acceleration to $1/6\,g$ lowers the Bond number and establishes a capillary-dominated hydraulic regime, with empirical flight data from Blue Origin NS-29 demonstrating suppressed capillary length ($L_c \approx 4.2\,\mathrm{mm}$) and boundary film thickening\cite{hasenstein2026assessing}. Coupled with intrinsic particle hydrophobicity (confirmed on Chang'e-5 samples\cite{zhang2025wettability}) and the absence of coarse aerating fractions in surface dust without multi-meter excavation\cite{mckay1991lunar,zhao2023variations,hasenstein2026assessing}, polydisperse regolith beds inevitably suffer severe capillary waterlogging, dry bypass channeling, and root-zone hypoxia\cite{ming1989lunar,ostrach1982low,hasenstein2026assessing,stout2001evidence}.

Transforming in-situ regolith into engineered sintered ceramics provides an ISRU manufacturing pathway that circumvents these edaphic constraints\cite{ming1989lunar,taylor2005microwave}. Thermal processing above $1050^\circ\mathrm{C}$ passivates reactive geochemical interfaces by solid-solubilizing $\mathrm{npFe}^0$ into stable augite and olivine crystal frameworks, rounds abrasive grain edges, and quenches dangling surface radicals, creating an inert, biocompatible growth substrate\cite{ming1989lunar,schoonen2020hydroxyl,xi2026multiphysics}. Pre-sintering classification of regolith particles to $45\text{--}75\,\mu\mathrm{m}$ produces monodisperse pore throat diameters of $30\text{--}60\,\mu\mathrm{m}$, stabilizing matric potential within the optimal window ($-2.5$ to $-5.0\,\mathrm{kPa}$) while maintaining air-filled porosity above $25\text{--}30\%$ ($D_p/D_o \approx 0.086 > 0.02$) for unhindered aerobic root respiration under fractional gravity\cite{ming1989lunar,heinse2007porous,jones2012beyond}. Furthermore, additive manufacturing of open-wedge capillary troughs modeled on ISS PWM-5 and PWM-6 fluidics enables dual-scale sintered architectures in which roots mediate gas--liquid phase separation without packed-bed pore collapse\cite{weislogel2026pwm,monje2026hydroponic}.

From a mission systems perspective, the upfront launch mass required for an autonomous sintering plant is offset by operational savings over multi-year horizons\cite{ming1989lunar,hanford2004advanced,metzger2023cost}. NASA BVAD-grounded Equivalent System Mass modeling normalized to edible yield demonstrates an operational breakeven of $9.4\,\mathrm{months}$ (nuclear) to $18\text{--}22\,\mathrm{months}$ (solar/RFC) against direct regolith cultivation, and $20.5\,\mathrm{months}$ against conventional Earth-imported hydroponics for a 6-person crew ($10.9\,\mathrm{months}$ for 12 crew)\cite{ming1989lunar,ewert2022bvad,colozza2020power}. Thus, conventional hydroponics or hybrid loops are advantageous for short sorties, whereas sintered ceramics provide $>98.5\%$ water recovery, intrinsic $\mathrm{pH}$ stability, and cyclic $500^\circ\mathrm{C}$ pyrolytic regeneration for permanent lunar settlements\cite{ming1989lunar,anderson2021lunar,barta1994thermal}.
